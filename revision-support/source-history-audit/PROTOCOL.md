# Raw-source diagnostic audit v1

The sample is frozen before annotation or comparison with extraction outcomes. Select two six-player puzzle IDs by the deterministic hash ranking in prepare.py, excluding puzzle IDs present in the known correction ledger or prior eight-example audit. Include all four team conditions for each puzzle: eight whole debates. This is a bounded pilot on two puzzle clusters from the previously unreviewed frame, not a representative corpus error estimate. The sample size is an effort bound, not a precision or acceptance threshold.

Annotators receive only neutral debate IDs, the puzzle statements and complete pre-final text fields. Model, condition, verified solution, extracted claims and recurrence outcomes are withheld. This is model-assisted reannotation, not independent human validation. Separate agents independently review initial fields; full-history annotators read every nonempty field in assigned debates.

First inventory every explicitly asserted or endorsed non-role proposition in the three initial explanations, whether correct or incorrect. Do not infer statement truth just from a knight/knave role assignment. A phrase such as "F is a knight making a true statement" DOES explicitly assert statement truth. Reports of what a puzzle character says are not assertions that the statement is true. Hypotheses and their scoped consequences are not unconditional commitments. Universal rules, tautologies, contradictions and vague proof summaries are recorded as exclusions or ambiguities. Count/SAME_ROLE expressions remain eligible; any expression containing a literal ROLE atom is excluded, including inside compounds. Split separately expressed propositions, preserve actually expressed logical structure, and do not add consequences solely because entailed.

Use the packaged compact expression vocabulary. Canonicalize explicit statement evaluations as STMT_TRUE(A) or NOT(STMT_TRUE(A)); SAME_ROLE(A,B), NOT,AND,OR,XOR,IFF,IMPLIES,COUNT and PARITY may be needed. Canonicalization/semantic grouping is an annotation choice; audit results must not be described as the same estimand as exact-expression matching without checking that distinction. Do not solve the puzzle to change an expressed claim. Truth/informativeness will be evaluated after annotation.

For every initially supplied target, inspect all three recipients' entire initial and later pre-final history. For each phase mark present, absent, or ambiguous. Present requires an unconditional assertion/endorsement of that proposition, with field ID and verbatim span. Independently derived statements still count as recurrence. A logical implication of a role assignment does not count as explicit expression. Record the first clear later match; read all later fields even when a match is found, to identify ambiguity/reversal relevant to interpretation. Absence requires scanning all relevant fields. Preserve ambiguous evidence instead of forcing a binary value. Equivalent explicit wording can match; a hypothesis cannot. Final decisions are excluded.

Each full-history output is JSON with debate_id, reviewed_field_ids, targets [{expression, initial: {agent-1:{status,evidence:[{field_id,span}],note},...}, later:{agent-1:...}}], exclusions [{field_id,span,reason}], and ambiguities. Include all agents for every target. Independent initial reviewer outputs initial_targets [{expression,agent,field_id,span,note}], exclusions and ambiguities, without seeing full-history reviewers' outputs. Freeze first-pass files before reconciliation or unblinding.

Compare raw-derived supply and source-verified initial/later decisions with the current translation inventory. Separate omitted supply, spurious supply, missed initial presence, false initial presence, missed later recurrence, false later recurrence, and expression-identity differences. Adjudicate discrepancies explicitly and retain original annotations. Report results by condition/tier, coverage and ambiguity, with sample-only contrasts where truth categories have nonzero denominators. Do not extrapolate error rates, significance, or a corrected 120-debate effect from eight audited debates. Do not modify the translator, corpus or main-paper estimates merely to fit the annotations.
